About the project

Media first. Partnerships measured.

Thor's Hall is a gaming media and partnership project founded by Arnar Thor in 2026. It combines original audience-building work with the campaign infrastructure creators and managers need to measure results.

What Thor's Hall does

Thor's Hall is building two assets at the same time. The first is an owned audience developed through gaming videos, short-form edits and live content. The second is a practical partnership system: dedicated campaign pages, source-specific links, application forms and reporting that connect an outcome to the partner who generated it.

The goal is not to collect a large list of inactive applicants or publish pages for the sake of page count. The goal is to find repeatable content formats, match the right campaign to the right adult audience and keep attribution clear enough that everyone can see what actually worked.

How a partnership is evaluated

Audience fit

We look at where the audience comes from, what content it already responds to and whether the proposed campaign belongs in that context.

Traffic quality

Real people and legitimate sources matter more than a large headline number. Bots, duplicate accounts, misleading claims and location bypassing are not accepted.

Compliance

Partners must follow the rules of their platform, the campaign operator and the jurisdictions in which their audience is located.

Measurement

Approved campaigns use a defined route so visits, outbound actions, applications and eligible results can be attributed consistently.

What Thor's Hall does not do

Thor's Hall is not a casino, sportsbook, payment processor or player-account support desk. It does not take deposits, operate games, set odds, approve withdrawals or control third-party accounts. When a campaign links to another service, that service's current terms and support process govern the user relationship.

Thor's Hall also does not promise a creator, manager or applicant that a campaign will earn money. Performance depends on audience fit, eligible activity, operator calculations and the written commercial terms for the campaign.

Commercial relationships

Some Thor's Hall pages and campaigns may contain affiliate or tracked links. If an eligible action is attributed to one of those links, Thor's Hall may receive commission. Approved creators or managers may receive a share under separate written terms.

That financial relationship is disclosed because it can create an incentive. It does not change product rules, legal eligibility, risk or the responsibility to verify current information with the service itself. See the Affiliate Disclosure for the full explanation.

Responsible boundaries

Some commercial campaigns may involve gambling or another age-restricted category. Those campaigns are intended only for eligible adults, must not target minors, and must not encourage anyone to spend money they cannot afford to lose. Availability varies by location.

Public gaming content and commercial campaign pages serve different purposes. Thor's Hall keeps dedicated campaign routes separate so their traffic and eligibility can be handled deliberately rather than blending every visitor into one funnel.

Current stage

Thor's Hall is early. The content library, creator network and reporting system are still being tested and improved. That stage is a constraint, but it also makes the operating principle simple: publish what is useful, measure what happens, keep what works and remove what does not.
