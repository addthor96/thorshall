About Thor's Hall

An independent Iceland-based Rainbet guide and creator partnership project.

Thor's Hall was founded by Arnar Þór in 2026. The project has two jobs: publish useful Rainbet information for users, and build tracked performance partnerships for creators, communities and managers.

Thor's Hall is independent. It is not Rainbet, does not operate gambling services and cannot control player accounts, deposits, withdrawals, KYC decisions or game outcomes.

What Thor's Hall actually does

Publishes focused guides

Country restrictions, KYC, deposits, withdrawals, bonus terms, platform reviews and comparisons are separated into distinct pages instead of mass-producing near-duplicate articles.

Builds creator campaigns

Approved partners can receive a custom landing page, tracked Rainbet campaign and private performance view so their traffic can be measured separately.

Runs performance partnerships

The standard creator model is based on affiliate commission actually received from tracked player activity, not a salary, fixed posting schedule or guaranteed payment.

The creator model

First 3 earning months: 100%

The creator receives 100% of the affiliate commission Thor's Hall actually receives from that creator's tracked campaign during the first three earning months.

Then: automatic 50/50

After the third earning month, the partnership automatically becomes a 50/50 split between the creator and Thor's Hall. Months with no earned affiliate commission do not consume an earning month.

No salary, retainer, fixed hours or guaranteed income is part of the standard partnership. Traffic must be real, adult, lawful and compliant with the operator's rules.

Editorial approach

Thor's Hall has an affiliate relationship with Rainbet, so complete neutrality would be the wrong claim. The goal instead is transparency: clearly disclose the relationship, verify facts against current operator information where possible, and avoid presenting affiliate preference as objective fact.

Facts vs opinion

Operator terms, restriction rules and account requirements should be identified as facts from the current source. Recommendations are presented as opinions.

Current over generic

Pages that depend on changing rules are updated when new information is available instead of being padded with evergreen filler.

Fewer, stronger pages

The site is being consolidated around a smaller set of pages with distinct intent, useful internal links and information that can be maintained.

Responsible promotion

Thor's Hall does not present gambling as income or a guaranteed way to make money. Public-facing promotion is intended for adults only. Partners are expected to avoid fake traffic, misleading claims, underage targeting, spam and account abuse.
