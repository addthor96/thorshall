How Verification Timing Usually Works

KYC approval is rarely instant when additional account review or document checking is required. Verification systems often move through several review stages before final approval.

STAGE 01

Document Upload

Identity documents, selfies, and proof-of-address files are submitted into the verification system queue.

STAGE 02

Automated Review

Verification systems first scan image clarity, expiration dates, formatting, and information consistency.

STAGE 03

Manual Verification Review

Some accounts require additional manual checks if documents trigger review flags or mismatches.

STAGE 04

Approval Or Retry Request

Users either receive approval confirmation or requests for updated documents and clearer uploads.

Most Common Reasons Verification Takes Longer

Low Quality Uploads

Blurry photos, cropped documents, glare, or unreadable information often trigger manual review delays.

Information Mismatch

Differences between account details, payment methods, and identity documents can extend review time.

High Verification Volume

Busy periods and increased account activity can slow down verification queues significantly.

Best Things Users Can Do
