Rainbet KYC Verification

Rainbet's current help center says KYC is not required in every case. The requirement depends on the payment method and whether Rainbet requests additional verification.

Updated: September 2026.

When does Rainbet require KYC?

  • Cryptocurrency transactions: Rainbet's KYC help article says KYC is not normally required simply to play or withdraw with crypto.
  • Interac: Rainbet says Interac transactions require verification and email verification.
  • Gift cards: the help article says KYC is not normally required.
  • Additional review: Rainbet can request more verification for suspicious activity or other compliance reasons.

How long does verification take?

Rainbet's public KYC article does not promise one fixed completion time for every review. Timing depends on what the account is being asked to verify and whether the submitted information is complete and readable. If Rainbet requests documents, follow the exact request shown in the account or sent by official support.
