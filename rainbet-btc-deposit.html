Rainbet BTC Deposit: What To Check First

Before sending Bitcoin, confirm that BTC is selected in the live Rainbet deposit screen and copy the address directly from that screen. Bitcoin transfers are irreversible, so the coin, network, address, and amount should all be checked before you approve the transaction in your wallet or exchange.

Open Rainbet wallet

Open the deposit area from your own account and generate or display the BTC deposit details there. Do not rely on an address copied from an old screenshot or message.

Choose Bitcoin / BTC

Make sure the selected asset is Bitcoin and that your sending wallet or exchange is preparing a Bitcoin-network withdrawal. Do not substitute another coin or network because an address looks similar.

Copy the exact BTC address

Use the copy button when available, then compare the first and last characters of the address before sending. A mistyped crypto address generally cannot be reversed.

BTC Deposit Safety Before You Send

Bitcoin transactions cannot normally be cancelled after broadcast. The safest process is to verify every detail before sending and to keep the transaction ID afterward so you can distinguish a blockchain delay from an account-crediting issue.

  • Use the BTC address displayed in your own live account
  • Confirm Bitcoin is the selected asset and network
  • Check the address after pasting it into the sending wallet
  • Review the fee and amount before approving the transfer
  • Save the transaction ID until the deposit is credited
