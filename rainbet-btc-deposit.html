How to deposit Bitcoin on Rainbet

  1. Log in and open the Rainbet wallet.
  2. Select the crypto deposit option.
  3. Select Bitcoin if it is available in your account.
  4. Copy the unique deposit address shown by Rainbet.
  5. In your external wallet or exchange, choose the matching asset/network.
  6. Paste the address and verify it again before sending.
  7. Send the transaction and keep the transaction ID (TXID).
  8. Wait for the required blockchain confirmation(s) and Rainbet crediting.

How long can a deposit take?

Rainbet says crypto-deposit timing depends on network traffic, network fees and the number of confirmations required for the specific asset. During congestion, a deposit can take longer than usual.
