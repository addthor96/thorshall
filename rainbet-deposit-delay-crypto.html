Wrong Network

Sending crypto on the wrong chain is one of the most serious deposit mistakes and may require support review.

Low Confirmations

A transaction can appear in a wallet explorer while still waiting for enough confirmations.

Exchange Delay

Some exchanges mark a withdrawal as sent before the receiving platform can fully credit it.
