How introductions are evaluated

Audience relevance

The creator's actual content and viewers must fit the proposed gaming campaign. A broad follower count is not enough.

Traffic quality

Thor's Hall does not accept bots, forced clicks, spam, duplicate accounts or misleading acquisition methods.

Campaign safety

Age restrictions, platform policies, advertising disclosures and location rules must be addressed before launch.

Submitting a creator does not guarantee approval, launch or earnings. Every accepted relationship is governed by written campaign terms.

Common questions

Is this employment?

No. The manager program is a performance-based commercial relationship, not a salaried role.

Is there a fixed override?

No universal rate is promised on this page. Any manager compensation is confirmed in writing for the specific partnership.

Can an agency submit several creators?

Yes. Submit the clearest initial fit first. A successful test is more useful than a long unqualified list.
