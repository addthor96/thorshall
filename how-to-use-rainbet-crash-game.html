Common Crash Game Mistakes

Waiting Too Long

Many beginners reach a good exit point, then lose the round because they chase a higher multiplier.

Raising Bets After Losses

Increasing bet size emotionally can drain a bankroll faster than the original losing round.

Believing Round Patterns

A few low or high crash results in a row does not create a guaranteed prediction for the next round.

Safer Crash Checklist

  • Start with smaller bet sizes while learning
  • Use auto cashout if manual timing feels emotional
  • Set a fixed session budget before playing
  • Avoid increasing bets just to recover losses
  • Take breaks after streaks, wins, or frustration
  • Treat crash as entertainment, not guaranteed income
