Processing Time vs Blockchain Time

A crypto withdrawal usually has two separate timing layers. First, the platform processes the withdrawal request. After approval, the blockchain network confirms the transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is processing time the same as blockchain confirmation time?
No. Processing time happens before the crypto transaction is fully broadcast, while confirmation time happens on the blockchain afterward.

Why is my withdrawal still processing?
Common reasons include account review, verification checks, withdrawal amount review, or payout queue timing.

What should I check first?
Check account status, wallet address, network selection, and whether any verification message was sent.
