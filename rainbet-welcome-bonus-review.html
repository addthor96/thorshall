Rainbet’s 40× welcome bonus: the maths before you accept it

The headline is a 100% first-deposit match. The important part is underneath it: Rainbet says the deposit and the bonus must be wagered 40 times. That turns a $100 deposit into an $8,000 wagering target.

Quick answer

As of 24 September 2026, Rainbet’s help centre lists one first-deposit promotion: a 100% match with free spins and a 40× wagering requirement calculated on the combined deposit and bonus. It can double the displayed play balance, but it also creates a large turnover requirement and temporary restrictions. It is not free cash.

What the current offer actually says

Rainbet’s official first-deposit article calls the offer the “40x Wager Locked Promotion.” A qualifying first deposit is matched 100%, and the deposit plus bonus is moved into a promotional balance. Rainbet also says the offer includes free spins for Sweet Bonanza.

The central condition is the turnover formula. Rainbet applies 40× to the combined amount, not only to the bonus. That difference matters more than the match percentage.

Your deposit$100
Matched bonus$100
Total play funds$200
$200 × 40 = $8,000 in qualifying wagers before the balance is unlocked.

That $8,000 figure is Rainbet’s own worked example. It does not mean a player must lose $8,000; it describes the cumulative eligible betting volume required. It also does not guarantee that any balance will remain after the wagering is completed.

The arithmetic is simple; the decision is not. A larger deposit produces a larger matched balance, but also a proportionally larger wagering obligation. Check the live promotion for any deposit minimum, match cap and currency-specific rules before funding an account.

Five restrictions the headline does not explain

1. The maximum bet is tied to the deposit

Rainbet’s deposit-bonus terms say bets made while rollover is active are capped at 1% of the deposit amount per bet, unless a specific promotion says otherwise. With a $100 deposit, that wording implies a $1 maximum bet while the lock is active.

2. Some high-RTP games are unavailable

The first-deposit article says games above 97.4% RTP are disabled during the promotion. The exact available catalogue can therefore differ from the normal lobby.

3. Withdrawals and transfers are restricted

The deposit-bonus terms state that withdrawals, vaulting and tipping in the affected currency are restricted during rollover. They also say the balance cannot be withdrawn until the requirement is completed or the rollover is forfeited where the rules allow it.

4. Starting play can remove the option to forfeit

Rainbet says rollover cannot be forfeited after play has started while an existing balance remains. In practical terms, decide whether the lock suits you before placing the first wager.

5. A second deposit can cancel eligibility

Rainbet’s general terms say the deposit-bonus promotion cannot be topped up with another deposit and that doing so forfeits eligibility and pending bonuses. The help centre also says first-deposit bonuses cannot be applied after the deposit has already been wagered or lost.

One clause needs clarification: Rainbet’s general Terms section 9.2 currently states a $50 maximum withdrawal for “welcome promo codes” and a $50 transfer limit for promotional or affiliate codes. The first-deposit help article does not explain how that clause applies to this deposit match. Ask Rainbet support for written confirmation before accepting the offer if the answer would affect your decision.

Bonus or no bonus?

The offer may suit someone who already intended to play for a long session, understands the eligible games and bet cap, and is comfortable locking the entire combined balance behind substantial wagering. It is a poor fit if quick withdrawal access or flexible bet sizing matters more than a larger starting balance.

Declining a bonus does not remove every account rule. Rainbet’s general terms state that crypto deposits carry a 1× wager lock, and identity checks can still be requested before withdrawal. The decision is therefore between different sets of conditions—not between conditions and no conditions.

  • Read the live offer inside your own account; promotion wording can change.
  • Confirm that gambling is legal where you are physically located.
  • Check the maximum qualifying deposit and eligible currencies.
  • Confirm the maximum bet and which games count toward rollover.
  • Ask support how the $50 clause in the general terms applies to your offer.
  • Save a copy of the terms shown when you opt in.
  • Only deposit an amount you can afford to lose completely.

Thor’s Hall verdict

The match is easy to understand; the lock is easy to underestimate. A 100% bonus sounds generous in isolation, but 40× the combined balance produces a high turnover target, and the 1% bet cap can make completing it slow. The unresolved wording around Rainbet’s $50 welcome-code clause is another reason to confirm the exact rules with support first.

Do not accept the offer because the headline number looks valuable. Accept it only if you have read the live terms, can explain the full wagering calculation yourself and would still be comfortable if the entire deposit were lost. Gambling is entertainment, not a way to earn income.
