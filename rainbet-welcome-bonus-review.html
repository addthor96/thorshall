What 40x combined balance means

The multiplier applies to the deposit plus the bonus, not only the bonus amount. That distinction makes the wagering requirement much larger than a 40x-bonus-only offer.

  • $50 deposit + $50 bonus = $100 promotional balance → $4,000 wagering.
  • $100 deposit + $100 bonus = $200 promotional balance → $8,000 wagering.
  • $250 deposit + $250 bonus = $500 promotional balance → $20,000 wagering.

These examples simply apply the 40x formula; check the live promotion screen for current minimums, maximums and game contribution rules.
