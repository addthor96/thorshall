Rainbet Solana Deposit Guide

Only use SOL if Solana is currently shown as a deposit option in your live Rainbet wallet. Payment options can change by account or region.

Updated: September 2026.

How to deposit SOL

  1. Open your Rainbet wallet and choose Crypto.
  2. Select Solana/SOL if it is currently available.
  3. Copy the exact deposit address shown in your account.
  4. In Phantom, an exchange or another wallet, choose SOL on the matching Solana network.
  5. Paste the Rainbet address and compare the beginning and end of the address before sending.
  6. Send the transfer and save the transaction signature.
  7. Wait for blockchain confirmation and Rainbet crediting.

Rainbet's general crypto-deposit instructions stress that the network selected in the sending wallet must match the network shown on Rainbet.

If a SOL deposit is delayed

  • Check whether the sending wallet or exchange created a transaction signature.
  • Check the transaction on a Solana explorer.
  • Confirm the destination matches the address Rainbet generated for your account.
  • If the blockchain shows the transfer as complete but Rainbet has not credited it, contact Rainbet support with the transaction signature.
  • If you used the wrong address or network, do not send another transfer until you understand what happened.

Should you send a test amount first?

For a new wallet route or large transfer, a small test transfer can reduce the amount at risk if an address or network was copied incorrectly. Always check any minimum-deposit rule shown in the live wallet first.
