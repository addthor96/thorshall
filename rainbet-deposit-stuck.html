Rainbet Deposit Stuck

A Rainbet deposit stuck issue means the crypto payment appears delayed somewhere between the sending wallet, blockchain confirmations, Rainbet deposit address, or account crediting system. This page focuses strictly on deposits that were sent but have not completed properly.

Deposit Stuck: Where The Problem Usually Is

Crypto deposits do not move in one single step. A stuck deposit usually sits in one of two places: wallet/network processing or platform crediting.

WALLET / BLOCKCHAIN SIDE

Transaction Not Fully Confirmed

The transaction may still be waiting for blockchain confirmations, exchange release, or network processing before Rainbet can credit it.

PLATFORM SIDE

Transaction Sent But Not Credited

If the transaction is confirmed on-chain, Rainbet may still need time to detect the payment and apply it to the correct account balance.

Information To Collect Before Support

Transaction Hash Needed to verify whether the payment exists on-chain.
Network Used Must match the network shown on the Rainbet deposit page.
Deposit Address Should match the address copied from the correct Rainbet account.
Confirmations Shows whether the blockchain has processed enough blocks.

Most Common Reasons A Deposit Gets Stuck

Wrong Network

Sending funds through the wrong chain is one of the biggest causes of stuck or missing crypto deposits.

Low Confirmations

The transaction may appear publicly before it has enough confirmations to be credited.

Exchange Hold

Some exchanges delay withdrawals even after showing partial completion inside the app.

Strict Checklist Before Assuming Funds Are Lost

  • Confirm the exact coin sent
  • Confirm the exact network selected
  • Open the transaction hash in a blockchain explorer
  • Check whether confirmations are still increasing
  • Verify the receiving deposit address
  • Check whether the sending exchange still marks it pending
