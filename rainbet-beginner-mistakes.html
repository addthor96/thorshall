Better Beginner Habits

Do This

  • Start with a small test deposit
  • Read bonus terms before claiming
  • Use fixed session limits
  • Learn one game at a time
  • Stop after emotional streaks

Avoid This

  • Depositing more because of hype
  • Changing bet size after every round
  • Trusting “guaranteed win” systems
  • Ignoring wallet network warnings
  • Playing tired or frustrated
