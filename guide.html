Practical guide

Know the checks before the click.

This guide covers the decisions that matter before opening an account, accepting a campaign promotion, or sending crypto to a gambling platform.

Start the checksAffiliate disclosure

1. Eligibility and account checks

AgeUse gambling services only if you meet the legal age requirement. Thor's Hall pages are 18+.
Local lawCheck whether the operator and the specific product are lawful where you are physically located.
Restricted locationsDo not use a VPN, proxy, or false information to bypass access controls or operator restrictions.
Identity verificationUse accurate details and expect the operator to request identity, source-of-funds, or address checks.

2. Promotion terms

The partner pages in this package retain their confirmed campaign-specific 250% first-deposit offer. That does not mean every visitor or deposit is eligible.

Use the intended routeOpen the offer through the correct partner page and `/join` campaign key.
Check selectionConfirm the promotion is displayed and selected during signup or deposit.
Read wagering rulesCheck the wagering multiple, qualifying games or bets, contribution rates, maximum bet, expiry, and withdrawal restrictions.
Live terms winIf the live operator screen differs from a Thor's Hall summary, rely on the current operator terms and ask support before depositing.

3. Crypto payment checks

Asset and networkThe token and network must match on both sides. A correct-looking address on the wrong network can still lose funds.
AddressCopy the current deposit address from the operator, verify the start and end, and never rely on an old screenshot.
Small testWhen practical, send a small test first and wait for confirmation before sending more.
RecordsSave the transaction ID, amount, network, time, and support correspondence if a transfer is delayed.

4. Responsible play

Gambling is not income and no strategy removes the house edge. Never borrow to gamble, chase losses, or treat a bonus as guaranteed value.

  • Set time and loss limits before playing.
  • Stop when the limit is reached.
  • Use cooling-off, exclusion, or account-limit tools when needed.
  • Do not promote gambling to minors or imply guaranteed winnings.
