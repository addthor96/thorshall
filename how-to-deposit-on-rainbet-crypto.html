Why Your Rainbet Deposit May Be Pending

Not Enough Confirmations

The transaction may be visible on-chain but not credited yet because it has not reached the required confirmations.

Exchange Delay

Some exchanges show a withdrawal as sent before the transaction is fully broadcast or confirmed.

Wrong Network Risk

If the wrong network was used, the deposit may not arrive normally and support may need to review it.

Common Crypto Deposit Mistakes To Avoid

  • Sending BTC, ETH, SOL, or USDT to the wrong network.
  • Forgetting a memo, tag, or extra field when the coin requires one.
  • Sending below the minimum deposit amount.
  • Copying an old address without checking the current Rainbet wallet screen.
  • Sending a large first deposit without testing a smaller amount.
  • Assuming an exchange withdrawal is complete before the blockchain confirms it.
