How Crypto Deposits Are Actually Processed

Many users think deposits are instant, but crypto deposits move through several technical stages before appearing in the account balance.

STEP 01

Wallet Sends

The exchange or wallet broadcasts the transaction onto the blockchain network.

STEP 02

Blockchain Confirmations

The network begins confirming the transaction through blockchain validation.

STEP 03

Platform Detection

Rainbet systems detect the deposit once confirmation requirements are met.

STEP 04

Balance Crediting

The deposit is finally credited into the account balance after processing completes.

Most Common Reasons Deposits Do Not Appear

Wrong Blockchain

Sending funds on the wrong network is one of the biggest causes of missing deposits.

Pending Confirmations

Transactions may exist publicly on-chain while still waiting for enough confirmations.

Exchange Delays

Some exchanges process withdrawals slower than users expect during busy periods.
