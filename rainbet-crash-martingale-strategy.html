How Martingale Systems Actually Work

Martingale strategies are based on increasing wager size after losses in an attempt to recover previous losses plus profit on a later win.

01

Start With A Base Bet

The strategy begins with a small wager size during early rounds.

02

Increase After Losses

After each losing round, the next wager is increased to recover prior losses if a win occurs.

03

Variance Eventually Matters

Long losing streaks can escalate wager size extremely fast, creating major bankroll pressure.

Main Martingale Risks

Crash games can produce streaks longer than users emotionally expect.
Bet sizing can escalate rapidly after several losses in a row.
