Rainbet Review: What the Public Terms Tell You

This is a document-based review of Rainbet's public Terms and help pages. It summarizes published operator, eligibility, verification and payment information; it is not a hands-on test of account signup, deposits, withdrawals or support response.

Rainbet Terms and help pages checked: 10 November 2026. This is a document-based review, not a hands-on test.

Operator and licence

Rainbet's current Terms identify Rain Group Ltd as the operator, with Anjouan company registration number 16077, and state that it is licensed in Anjouan to provide online games of chance.

Rainbet Terms

Country restrictions

Rainbet currently lists the United States, France, the Netherlands and specified territories, Australia, Austria, Germany, the United Kingdom and Northern Ireland, and Spain among its restricted jurisdictions. The terms also prohibit using a VPN or proxy to disguise a user's real location.

Rainbet supported countries guide

KYC

Rainbet's KYC help article says crypto transactions do not require KYC to play or withdraw, while Interac requires KYC Level 2 and email verification. Its Terms separately reserve the right to request identity and location documentation.

Rainbet KYC help · KYC guide

Crypto deposits

Rainbet's crypto deposit guide says users choose the cryptocurrency, use the unique deposit address generated for the account and select the matching blockchain network. A network mismatch can cause lost funds.

Official crypto deposit guide

Withdrawals and account checks

Rainbet says transaction processing is typically 5–15 minutes; crypto confirmation is separate, with published estimates of 5–45 minutes and up to 45–60 minutes for Bitcoin during fee spikes. Its Terms and help pages also describe account restrictions for fraud, promotional abuse, AML checks or age verification. See the withdrawal-time guide and Rainbet's withdrawal-time help.

What this review can—and cannot—verify

Covered by public documents

The linked Terms and help pages describe Rainbet’s named operator and licence, listed location restrictions, some verification conditions, crypto deposit steps and withdrawal/account rules.

Not independently tested here

We did not test account signup, deposits, withdrawals, gameplay or support response times. This review therefore makes no firsthand claim about payout speed, service quality or game experience.
