Beginner Mistakes To Avoid

Claiming Every Bonus

Not every offer fits every player. Some promos create more pressure than value.

Betting Too Large Early

Large bets can erase the bankroll before the user even understands the promo rules.

Ignoring Expiry Rules

A bonus can become useless if the user cannot complete requirements before the timer ends.

Simple Beginner Checklist

  • Read wagering, expiry, and cashout terms before claiming
  • Check minimum deposit and eligible games
  • Use a bankroll size that still feels safe without the bonus
  • Avoid chasing losses to finish requirements
  • Track progress instead of guessing
  • Skip promos that do not fit your play style
