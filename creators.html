How the revenue share works

First 3 earning months: 100%

An earning month is a month in which your tracked campaign generates affiliate commission payable to Thor's Hall. During your first three earning months, Thor's Hall passes on 100% of the affiliate commission it actually receives from your campaign.

After that: automatic 50/50

After your third earning month, the arrangement automatically becomes 50% to you and 50% to Thor's Hall. It is not a temporary trial that requires renegotiation to continue.

Monthly settlement

Creator payments are settled after the operator completes the applicable affiliate billing period and Thor's Hall receives the commission. Payment method is agreed with the creator.

No guaranteed earnings

Revenue depends on tracked users, player activity, operator calculations and account eligibility. There is no salary, guaranteed payout or guaranteed number of conversions.

Creator FAQ

Do I need thousands of followers?

No. A smaller audience with real trust and engagement can be more useful than a large inactive following.

How much do I receive?

For your first 3 earning months, you receive 100% of the affiliate commission Thor's Hall actually receives from your tracked campaign. After your third earning month, the split automatically becomes 50/50.

What counts as an earning month?

A month only counts when your campaign generates affiliate commission payable to Thor's Hall. A month with no affiliate commission does not use one of your first three earning months.

Is there a salary, posting requirement or fixed schedule?

No. The standard partnership is performance-based. There is no salary, retainer, fixed work schedule or minimum posting quota.

Is revenue guaranteed?

No. Revenue depends on traffic quality, conversions, player activity, operator calculations and tracking. No creator income or gambling result is guaranteed.
