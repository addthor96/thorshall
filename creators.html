Revenue terms

First 3 earning months

For qualifying campaigns, you receive 100% of the affiliate commission Thor's Hall actually receives from your tracked route. A month only counts when commission is payable to Thor's Hall.

After the third earning month

The standard split becomes 50% to you and 50% to Thor's Hall unless different written terms have been agreed for that campaign.

Monthly settlement

Payments are calculated after the relevant platform completes its billing period and Thor's Hall receives the applicable commission.

No guaranteed earnings

Revenue depends on eligible traffic, attributed activity, platform calculations and compliance. A page or click does not guarantee a payment.

Who can apply?

Audience evidence

A compliance plan

State how you will handle age restrictions, disclosures, platform rules and location limits.

Questions before applying

Is this a salaried role?

No. It is a performance-based commercial partnership. There are no fixed hours, posting quotas or guaranteed payments.

Do I need a huge audience?

No fixed minimum applies. A small, engaged and relevant audience can be more useful than a large audience with weak intent.

Can I use paid or automated traffic?

Only traffic methods approved in writing for the campaign may be used. Bots, forced clicks, spam and misleading acquisition are prohibited.

When does a month count?

An earning month counts only when the campaign generates commission that becomes payable to Thor's Hall.
