What is this ledger?

This ledger shows tracked Thor's Hall partner activity where public data is available.

Wagered = total amount wagered by tracked users.

Deposits = total deposits tracked through the partner link.

Visits = users who visited through the tracked campaign.

Registrations = accounts created through the tracked campaign.

NGR = net gaming revenue after wins, bonuses, and adjustments where available.

This ledger shows tracked all-time activity, not monthly payout amounts. Creator payouts are calculated from Thor's Hall's final received affiliate commission for the payout period, not directly from the public NGR total shown here.

Some rows may be private, reserved, or waiting for activity. Public partner rows are only shown when the creator or manager agrees to be listed.
