How long does a crypto withdrawal take?

Rainbet separates platform processing from blockchain confirmation. Its help article says internal processing is typically 5–15 minutes. Depending on the coin, network load and fee type, blockchain confirmation may take roughly 5–45 minutes, with occasional longer delays during fee spikes.

Rainbet's transaction-issues guide says support can assist with failed requests and, in some cases, cancel the failed transaction.

Wrong address

Once a crypto transfer is confirmed to an incorrect address, recovery may not be possible. Always verify the address before submitting.

Account restrictions can stop withdrawals

Rainbet says betting, tipping or withdrawals may be restricted for suspected promotional abuse, fraud, AML reviews, age verification or other Terms violations. If the account shows a restriction error, contact official support rather than repeatedly submitting requests.
