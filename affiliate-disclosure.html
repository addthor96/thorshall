Creator and manager partnerships

Thor's Hall may share commission with an approved creator, community owner, manager or agency under separate written terms. A partner's share is calculated from commission actually received by Thor's Hall for the applicable tracked campaign, unless the written agreement states otherwise.

These are performance-based commercial partnerships. They are not salaried roles, and traffic, clicks or content output do not guarantee earnings.
