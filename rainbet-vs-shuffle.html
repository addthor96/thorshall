5. Withdrawal and Deposit Rollover

Both use 1x raw-deposit rollover

6. Bonuses Are Separate From the 1x Deposit Rule

Do not confuse the normal 1x raw-deposit requirement with a promotional bonus rollover. A bonus can carry much larger wagering requirements. Shuffle, for example, currently documents a 100% first-deposit promotion with a 35x rollover on deposit + bonus under that specific promotion.

Rainbet promotions also have their own terms. Always read the exact offer active on your account before claiming it.
