Licence and operator

Rainbet's current Terms identify RBGAMING N.V. and state that it is licensed in Anjouan. Shuffle's Terms identify Natural Nine B.V. and show Curaçao licence OGL/2024/1337/0628.

KYC difference

Rainbet's public KYC article says cryptocurrency transactions do not require KYC in every case, although additional verification can be requested. Shuffle's Terms are broader: Shuffle reserves the right to ask for KYC documentation at any time and may restrict access, payments or withdrawals until verification is satisfactory.

Crypto support

Shuffle publishes a detailed list of supported assets and networks in its help center. Rainbet's generic crypto guide tells users to choose the asset and matching network displayed in the live wallet. For either site, never assume a token or blockchain is supported because another operator supports it.

Which should you choose?

First eliminate any operator that is not permitted in your real location. Then compare the current KYC requirement, crypto asset/network you actually use, promotion terms and withdrawal rules. Those practical differences matter more than a generic affiliate score.
