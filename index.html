What Thor's Hall is building

The project has two connected parts: an audience built through original gaming content, and an operating layer that lets approved partners run measurable campaigns without creating the technical system themselves.

Gaming media

Short-form videos, longer edits and live content designed to earn attention through entertainment rather than recycled promotional pages.

Creator infrastructure

Custom landing pages, source-specific routes, application forms and private reporting for approved partners.

Performance partnerships

Campaigns are evaluated on real, attributable outcomes. Traffic volume alone is not treated as success.

How the system works

1

Audience fit

We review the channel, audience, traffic source and campaign idea before anything goes live.

2

Dedicated route

An approved partner receives a page and campaign route tied to that source.

3

Measured actions

Visits, outbound clicks, applications and eligible campaign results are attributed consistently.

4

Reporting

Partners receive reporting based on recorded results and the written terms for their campaign.

Built for evidence, not promises

Thor's Hall is early-stage. We do not promise reach, earnings or conversion rates. We do promise that approved campaigns are set up with a defined source, a traceable route and terms that explain how eligible results are handled.

Age-restricted campaigns: Some commercial partnerships may involve regulated or age-restricted gaming services. Those campaigns use dedicated pages, are intended only for eligible adults, and must follow operator, platform and local rules.
