THOR'S HALL — GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE BRIDGE

Functions added:
- /.netlify/functions/gsc-auth-start
- /.netlify/functions/gsc-oauth-callback
- /.netlify/functions/gsc-report

Required Netlify environment variables:
- GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID
- GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET
- GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI
- GSC_OAUTH_STATE_SECRET
- GSC_REFRESH_TOKEN (added after authorization)

Optional:
- GSC_SITE_URL (defaults to sc-domain:thorshall.gg)
- GSC_REPORT_KEY (when set, gsc-report requires ?key=VALUE or x-gsc-key header)

Important:
Before authorizing, move the Google OAuth app out of Testing and into Production. Google refresh tokens for an External app left in Testing expire after 7 days when non-basic scopes are requested.

After the production deploy:
1. Open https://thorshall.gg/.netlify/functions/gsc-auth-start
2. Approve read-only Search Console access.
3. Copy the returned refresh token into Netlify as GSC_REFRESH_TOKEN.
4. Trigger a new production deploy.
5. Open https://thorshall.gg/.netlify/functions/gsc-report
6. Optional ranges: ?days=7, ?days=28, or ?days=90

The bridge is read-only. It does not submit sitemaps, request indexing, modify properties, or change Search Console settings.
