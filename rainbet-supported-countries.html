Does Rainbet allow a VPN?

No. Rainbet's Terms section 3.4 says users may not use a VPN, proxy or similar service or device that masks or manipulates identification of their real location. A VPN should not be used to bypass a country restriction.

Can you use Rainbet in Canada?

Canada is not named in Rainbet's current operator restricted-country list. That does not automatically mean every Canadian user can legally use every Rainbet product or payment method. Gambling law and availability can vary by province, and the user remains responsible for checking the rules that apply to their real location.

For example, the fact that a site loads or allows registration is not proof that a particular gambling activity is legally available in a province.

How to check your country

  1. Open Rainbet's current Terms and Conditions.
  2. Check the restricted-jurisdiction section against your real country and location.
  3. Check the law or regulator guidance that applies where you live.
  4. Do not use a VPN or false location information to bypass a restriction.
  5. If the operator's terms and local rules conflict, do not deposit until you have a clear answer.
