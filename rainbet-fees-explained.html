How To Reduce Fee Friction

Use Efficient Networks

Cheaper or faster chains may reduce transfer costs, but only if the platform and wallet support the exact network.

Avoid Tiny Transfers

Frequent small withdrawals can make fixed fees feel much more expensive than planned.

Check Before Sending

Fees can change during congestion, so users should confirm cost before final wallet approval.

Fee Mistakes Users Make

  • Assuming every crypto transfer has the same cost
  • Ignoring exchange withdrawal fees
  • Sending very small amounts during expensive network periods
  • Choosing a cheap chain without checking support
  • Forgetting conversion spreads when buying crypto
  • Confusing platform fees with blockchain fees
