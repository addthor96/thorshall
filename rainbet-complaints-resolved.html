What “Resolved Complaint” Actually Means

A resolved complaint does not always mean the user got exactly what they wanted. It usually means the issue reached a clear outcome: payout processed, verification completed, support explained the decision, or account status was clarified.

STRONG RESOLUTION SIGNALS

Signs A Complaint Was Handled Properly

Support gave a clear reason for the issue.
The user received a final account or payout update.
Verification steps were explained instead of ignored.
The same issue does not appear repeatedly across many users.
WEAK RESOLUTION SIGNALS

Signs Users Should Review Carefully

No clear reason was provided for a delay.
Multiple users report the same unresolved pattern.
The complaint lacks transaction, verification, or account context.
The user only shares screenshots without the final outcome.
