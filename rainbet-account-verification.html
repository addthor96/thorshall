Good Verification Setup Vs Bad Setup

Good Setup

  • Account details are accurate and consistent
  • Documents, if requested, are clear and readable
  • Country and age eligibility are checked early
  • Withdrawal information matches the account flow
  • Users respond calmly if review is needed

Bad Setup

  • Using mismatched names or account details
  • Uploading blurry or cropped documents
  • Waiting until a large withdrawal to fix account issues
  • Trying to rush support without providing needed info
  • Ignoring terms, region rules, or verification prompts

Common Verification Delays

Unclear Document Uploads

Blurry images, cropped corners, expired documents, or unreadable text can slow reviews.

Mismatched Account Details

Different names, regions, or profile information can create extra checks before approval.

Withdrawal Timing Pressure

Users often notice verification only when they want a payout, which makes the process feel more stressful.

Before Verification Starts

  • Use accurate account information from the beginning
  • Check local legality and age requirements before signup
  • Keep documents clear, current, and readable if requested
  • Do not create multiple accounts to bypass checks
  • Read bonus, withdrawal, and verification terms carefully
  • Complete review steps before expecting large withdrawals to move instantly
