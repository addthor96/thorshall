Example 1 of 4 · overtime

The map reaches 12–12, then Team A wins in overtime

A final map winner and a regular-time draw can both be meaningful, depending on the ticket's market.

Rainbet

“X map winner”

The published definition includes overtime, so it refers to the eventual map winner in this example.

“X map – will there be overtime”

This asks whether overtime is required to determine the map winner.

Source: Sportsbook Rules, esports 1.9; General markets “X map winner” and “will there be overtime.”

Stake

“Map winner 2-way”

This is the team that wins the completed map.

“Map winner 3-way”

At 12–12 after regulation, its published definition makes the draw the winning selection, even if a team later wins overtime.

Source: Sportsbook Rules, Counter-Strike 2 → Markets “Map winner 2-way / 3-way.”

Ticket check: “2-way” and “3-way” on Stake are different markets. Do not use the final OT winner to read a regular-time 3-way ticket.

Example 2 of 4 · score

Regulation ends 12–12; overtime adds more rounds

A correct-score market and a total-rounds market do not necessarily use the same endpoint.

Rainbet

“X map – correct score”

Its market definition excludes overtime. A 12–12 regulation score remains the reference score for that named market.

“X map – total rounds”

Its definition includes overtime. The total is greater than the 24 rounds played in regulation.

Source: Sportsbook Rules, General markets “X map – correct score” and “X map – total rounds.”

Stake

“Number of Rounds”

The CS2 market asks for the count of rounds played on a map; its general esports rule includes overtime unless a market says otherwise.

“Map Side Total Rounds”

Its CS2 rule expressly excludes overtime rounds for side totals such as “Terrorist Total Rounds.”

Source: Sportsbook Rules, Esports General and Counter-Strike 2 → General rules / Markets.

Ticket check: Check whether it says map total, side total or correct score. The label changes which rounds count.

Example 3 of 4 · format

The organizer changes a BO5 to a BO3

Assume the format change is official, not a typo or an unconfirmed rumor.

Rainbet

Map-level markets

Its published format-change rule says these remain valid.

Match-level markets

The rule says these are voided, including Match Winner and Map Handicap.

Source: Sportsbook Rules, esports 1.10.

Stake

Affected bets

Its published rule reserves the right to void all bets when the match format changes or differs from the offered format. It does not promise an automatic outcome for a specific ticket.

Source: Sportsbook Rules, Esports General, format change.

Ticket check: Identify whether you chose a map-level or match-level market, then check the operator's event decision.

Example 4 of 4 · roster

One organizer-approved stand-in plays in a CS2 match

Assume the match produces an official result and no other exceptional event occurs.

Rainbet

Relevant markets

Its published esports rule says they stand with an organizer-approved stand-in and official result.

Large roster change

If more than 50% of the starting roster is replaced, its rule says bets on markets involving the match are void.

Source: Sportsbook Rules, esports 1.6.

Stake

Relevant markets

Its published esports rule says bets are resulted normally for permitted stand-ins with an official result.

Large roster change

If at least 50% of the team roster is replaced via stand-ins, its rule says bets are void and a new fixture is created.

Source: Sportsbook Rules, Esports General, stand-ins.

Ticket check: A player-specific market may have separate substitution rules. Confirm whether your ticket concerns the team or a named player.
